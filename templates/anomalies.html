About Anomaly Detection

The Isolation Forest algorithm isolates observations using random partitioning. Observations requiring fewer splits to isolate are considered anomalies. An anomaly does not necessarily indicate an attack — it represents a behavioral pattern that deviates significantly from normal network traffic baselines. Anomaly detection is most effective as a complementary layer to supervised threat classification.
